Let me introduce myself

Jelle van der Wal

I have been tutoring since 2016: at various schools, in students' homes and now fully online. From 2022 to 2025 I taught Public Administration seminars at Leiden University, and since 2025 I have been a PhD candidate in Political Science at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB). I live in Spain, which is why all my lessons are online.

Every student is different. That is why I first find out where the difficulty lies and then tailor my explanations and exercises to it. My goal: better results, and learning with more enjoyment and interest.

  • Bachelor in International Studies (Leiden University and UFSCar, Brazil)
  • Master's degrees in Public Administration and Latin American Studies
  • Languages: Dutch (native), English, Portuguese and Spanish
  • Extensive experience with Dutch as a second language (NT2) and the International Transition Class (ISK)
